## App name

Planes Above Me

## Short description / tagline

What plane is that? Point your wrist at an aircraft and see where it is flying.

## Category

Watch App, store category **Travel** (or Outdoor, if Travel is not offered)

## Description

Point your wrist at an aircraft in the sky and find out what it is. Planes Above Me shows the
live air traffic around you on a compass. For the aircraft you are facing, it shows its
destination, aircraft type, altitude, speed and operator.

It finds anything that broadcasts ADS-B: airliners, small airplanes, helicopters, business
jets, cargo and military flights.

WHAT YOU SEE
• A compass radar with every aircraft within range, drawn as a triangle pointing along its
  course, in all directions.
• The aircraft in front of you, large: callsign, route (for example "WAW/PL -> LHR/GB London"),
  altitude, type and operator, speed and distance. An arrow shows where to look and turns
  green when you face it.
• A detail page with origin and destination airports, manufacturer and type, operator,
  registration, country, altitude, speed and course.
• Lock: follow one aircraft across the sky; the arrow keeps pointing at it as you turn.
• A list of all aircraft nearby, nearest first.

SETTINGS
Search radius (default 10 km) and refresh interval (default 30 s), in the menu on the watch
(swipe in from the right edge) or in the Connect IQ settings on your phone.

REQUIREMENTS
• A Garmin watch with GPS. "What you're facing" needs a compass (magnetometer).
• A paired phone with Garmin Connect and an internet connection. Live flight data comes from
  the internet.

## How to use (short guide for the description or the support page)

1. Start Planes Above Me outdoors and wait for GPS.
2. Point the watch at the aircraft you see. The one in front of you is shown large.
3. Tap the screen (or press Start) for the detail page. Start on the detail page locks the
   aircraft, so the arrow follows it.
4. Swipe up for the list of all aircraft nearby. Swipe in from the right edge for the menu
   (refresh, search radius, refresh interval, About).

## FAQ (support page)

**It shows an error on the status line.**
The app needs a connection to your phone with Garmin Connect running and internet access.

**An aircraft I can see is not shown.**
Only aircraft that broadcast ADS-B and are received by the OpenSky Network appear. Some small
or military aircraft do not broadcast, and positions can be a few seconds old.

**The route is wrong.**
Routes are looked up by callsign and can be out of date. On the detail page open the menu and
choose "Re-check route" to look it up again from a second source.

**The arrow points the wrong way.**
Calibrate the compass in the watch settings (Sensors → Compass), away from metal.

## Privacy

Planes Above Me does not collect or store any data on a server of its own. To find aircraft,
the watch sends an area around your position (through the paired phone) to the OpenSky
Network. To show type and route, it sends the aircraft's transponder code and callsign to
adsbdb.com and, when re-checking a route, to hexdb.io. The developer does not receive your
position.

## Publishing notes

- Free app. The OpenSky Network allows its data only for non-commercial use, so the app must
  stay free (a paid version would need an OpenSky licence or another data source).
- Upload `PlanesAboveMe.iq` as a **new app**. Do not tick "Beta".
- The store package is built by GitHub Actions for tags `v*`
  (`git tag v1.0.0 && git push --tags`), artifact `PlanesAboveMe-store-package`. Tag builds
  fail without the `CIQ_DEVELOPER_KEY` secret, so every release is signed with the same key.
- Anonymous OpenSky access has a daily request budget per IP address (the phone's), so each
  user has their own budget; the default refresh of 30 s stays well within it.
