Privacy policy

Planes Above Me for Garmin watches

Planes Above Me does not collect or store any data on a server of its own. The developer does not receive your position or any other data.

Data sent to third-party services

The watch sends its requests through the paired phone (Garmin Connect) to these public services:

  • The OpenSky Network: an area around your position, to find the aircraft nearby.
  • adsbdb.com: the transponder code and callsign of the aircraft you are looking at, to show its type and route.
  • hexdb.io: the callsign and airport codes, when a route is checked again (automatically when it looks out of date, or on request).

These services receive the request and your phone's IP address and handle them according to their own privacy policies.

Location and sensors

The GPS position and the compass are used on the watch to show where the aircraft are relative to you. Apart from the search area sent to the OpenSky Network, your position does not leave the watch and is not stored by the app.

Settings

Your settings (search radius, refresh interval) are stored on the watch and in the Connect IQ app on your phone.

Advertising and tracking

The app contains no advertising, analytics or tracking.
